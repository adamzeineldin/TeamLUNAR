Research

How do we move cargo
across the Moon?

We aim to design and test a rover that will be able to effectively transport materials and facilitate exploration of the Moon.

UMD Gemstone Honors College

The need

With NASA’s plan to build a lunar base in the near future, a method of transportation around the Moon and to the base is needed.

We aim to design and create a lunar rover capable of payload transportation and recovery.

The problem

100%

of rovers are not recovered after missions.

Rovers often get stuck navigating the uneven terrain of the Moon.
